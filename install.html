Pulse

Ad-free, account-free music player

Install with SideStore
  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Make sure LocalDevVPN is connected and you're on Wi-Fi.
  3. Tap Install with SideStore, then Open.
  4. Wait for SideStore to finish. Pulse appears on your Home Screen.
Or download Pulse.ipa and add it in SideStore with the + button.
